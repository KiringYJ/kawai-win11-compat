The original readme identifies Kawai2004 v1.01 as `FREEWARE` and names the
author as 陳一進. The local 3,577-byte reference readme has been
transformed and must not be presented as the byte-identical archive readme.

The permission statement verifies that the author invited downloading, free
use, copying, and distribution of the software. It does not establish the
provenance or permissions of every embedded work. The rendered title and board
contain recognizable Pokémon character artwork, and the game includes music or
audio whose rights were not independently verified in this audit. This is an
unresolved evidence boundary, not an accusation that the author lacked
permission.

`fmod.dll` separately requires a Firelight license analysis even though its
bytes were included in the author-published ZIP.
