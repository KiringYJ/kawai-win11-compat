# Shared Kawai author permission evidence

This file records the author's site-wide permission statement for the three
Kawai games used by this project. Per-game files record the exact publication
artifacts and remaining third-party-content boundaries.

Archived source:
<https://web.archive.org/web/20080604014823id_/http://cps-chen.myweb.hinet.net/public/menu_pc.htm>

The Big5/CP950 page lists all three works:

- KAWAI 連連看 2003 練習版, dated June 2003;
- KAWAI 連連看 2004, dated November 2003; and
- KAWAI 連連看 2005, dated February 2005.

Original permission statement:

> 本站所提供的個人創作軟體作品歡迎網友下載自由使用、複製、散佈。

English translation for this evidence record:

> Visitors are welcome to download, freely use, copy, and distribute the
> personally created software works provided on this site.

The page follows this with a no-warranty statement and places operational risk
on the user.

This evidence verifies the author's invitation to copy and distribute the
listed software. It does not establish the provenance or permissions of every
artwork, music, sound, library, or Microsoft runtime used by those programs.
That is an evidence boundary, not a finding that the author lacked permission.
See the per-game evidence files and `docs/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` before
conveying a binary.
