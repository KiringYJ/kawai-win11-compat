Firelight's archived 2003 licensing page states that FMOD may be used free in
a product that is not intended to make money and is not charged for. The
current Firelight EULA expressly names FMOD 3 as a runtime engine that may be
integrated and redistributed in a non-commercial product when its conditions
are met. Those conditions include no monetisation, sponsorship, or promotion,
and a Firelight credit.

- contemporaneous page:
  <https://web.archive.org/web/20031008115624id_/http://fmod.org/fmodlicense.html>
- current EULA: <https://www.fmod.com/legal>

Required credit for any route relying on the current terms:

```text
FMOD Sound System by Firelight Technologies Pty Ltd.
```

This evidence does not clear commercial distribution, standalone distribution
of `fmod.dll`, redistribution as a tool set, or any release involving charges,
monetisation, sponsorship, or promotion. The historical archive did not ship
an FMOD license file, and no project-specific confirmation from Firelight has
been obtained. See `docs/REDISTRIBUTION.md` before any public release.
